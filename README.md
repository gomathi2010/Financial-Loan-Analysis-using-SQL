# Financial-Loan-Analysis-using-SQL

This project leverages SQL queries to analyze financial loan data, with a focus on key performance indicators (KPIs) and the status of loans issued. The analysis includes the total number of applications, loan amounts funded, payments received, interest rates, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios, segmented by month, loan status, and other borrower characteristics. The goal of this project is to provide insights into the loan portfolio's performance and help in evaluating good and bad loans, along with their associated financial metrics.

**Key Insights and Queries**

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Total Applications: The total number of loan applications, with monthly breakdowns.

Total Funded Amount: The total loan amount funded, with monthly breakdowns.

Total Amount Collected: The total payments collected for loans, including monthly analysis.

Average Interest Rate: The average interest rate for all loans, including month-to-date (MTD) and previous month-to-date (PMTD) breakdowns.

Average DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio): The average DTI ratio, with monthly comparisons.

**Good Loan Issued**

Good Loan Percentage: Percentage of loans marked as 'Fully Paid' or 'Current' out of all loans.

Total Funded and Amount Collected: Analysis of funded amounts and payments collected for 'Good Loans' (loans that are paid or current).

**Bad Loan Issued**

Bad Loan Percentage: Percentage of loans that were 'Charged Off' out of all loans.

Total Funded and Amount Collected: Breakdown of funded amounts and payments collected for 'Bad Loans'.

**Loan Status Analysis**

Loan Status Breakdown: Summary of loan status (Fully Paid, Current, Charged Off, etc.), showing the number of loans, total payments received, total loan amounts funded, and average interest rate and DTI for each status.

MTD Loan Status: Analyzes the loan statuses for the current month to date (MTD), showing funded amounts and total payments received.

**Bank Loan Report**

Loan Trends by Month: Provides a monthly breakdown of loan applications, total funded amounts, and total payments received.

Loan Trends by State: Analyzes loan data by state, including the number of applications and total amounts funded and collected.

Loan Trends by Term: Analyzes loan data based on loan term, providing insights into funding and repayment across different term lengths.

Loan Trends by Employment Length: Breaks down loans based on borrower’s employment length, providing insights into the relationship between employment history and loan applications.

Loan Trends by Purpose: Breaks down loans based on their purpose (e.g., debt consolidation, home improvement, etc.), offering insights into why people take out loans.

Loan Trends by Home Ownership: Analyzes loans based on homeownership status (e.g., owner, renter), helping to assess trends based on borrower property ownership.

**Conclusion**

This SQL analysis project provides an in-depth view of the financial loan portfolio, allowing stakeholders to assess loan performance and trends based on various dimensions. Key findings include:

Good Loans (Fully Paid or Current) are more common, with significant funds being received.

Bad Loans (Charged Off) account for a smaller percentage of the portfolio, but they still represent a risk that needs attention.

The analysis of loans by month, state, term, employment length, purpose, and home ownership offers valuable insights into loan performance across different segments.

Interest rates and DTI ratios are crucial in determining loan quality, and their averages provide insights into the risk profile of the loan portfolio.

This project can help financial institutions understand the health of their loan portfolio, track payment trends, and identify areas for improvement in their lending practices.
